Christy Li

I am an undergrad at MIT studying CS and Physics. Currently, I'm researching automated harm remediation for computer use agents as a Research Scholar at ML Alignment and Theory Scholars (MATS). I am broadly interested in building AI agents that can learn and adapt through interactions with their environment and users to effectively collaborate with humans on complex, long-horizon tasks.

In my free time, I like to read, play volleyball, and slowly but surely taste test every matcha drink in Boston. If you'd like to chat about any of the above or just want to say hi, feel free to reach out!
